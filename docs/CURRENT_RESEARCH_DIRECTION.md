# Current Research Direction

As of 2026-08-18, governed by the Balanced Research Handoff v5 (`handoff/00`–`10`; `11`–`15` are a non-authoritative notebook).

- The exact Lean-backed MathRecord core (Gates 0–1, `reports/`, `SCHEMA.md`, ADR-0001) is preserved unchanged and remains the only formal source of truth.
- **No final MathMap ontology has been selected.** Named-declaration nodes, AND–OR hypergraphs, selective zoom, and use-event schemas are hypotheses, not architecture.
- **Phase 2A** (current): extract a bounded heterogeneous real Mathlib corpus (500–2,000 declarations, ≥4 areas, 40–80 showcase proofs) and derive/compare candidate projections P0–P6 of the same proofs, with provenance, trust classes, and quantitative characterization.
- **Phase 2B** (current): a feasibility measurement of contextual named-declaration use events (explicit families only: `apply`/`exact`/`refine`/`rw`/`unfold`/constructors/explicit simp lemmas), reporting precision, coverage, and ambiguity — not a full event ontology.
- Model training happens only after a representation is selected, in a later controlled navigation experiment.
- This run ends with exactly one recommendation: proceed with a selected representation / run another bounded study / stop the map-centered program.
- Claims discipline: every reported claim is labeled reproduced-evidence, implementation result, deterministic projection, human annotation, model inference, design judgment, or future hypothesis. Human usefulness is not claimed without human review.
