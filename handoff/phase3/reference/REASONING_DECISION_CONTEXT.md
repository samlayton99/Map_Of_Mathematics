# Reference — Reasoning Decision Context

## Why this phase was selected

The exact record is now credible enough on the tested corpus to support derived experiments. The highest-value unresolved question is whether its structure contains a signal that separates formal scaffolding from mathematical landmarks.

A purely manual proof-route review was previously recommended, but independent human reviewers will not be recruited. The new study therefore combines:

- a structural separability experiment;
- a small user vibe-check;
- independent agent review where available;
- explicit proxy labels and disagreement analysis.

## Strong claims to avoid

Do not assume:

- that “machinery” is an objective permanent node class;
- that high global centrality means human importance;
- that low centrality means uselessness;
- that filtering infrastructure automatically reveals the proof’s conceptual core;
- that one global graph score can replace state-conditioned retrieval;
- that graph communities equal mathematical fields;
- that a classifier reproducing P3 validates P3;
- that a visually persuasive graph is useful.

## Strong insights to preserve

- The raw elaborated graph contains a great deal of formal machinery.
- The machinery may have recurring structural signatures.
- Mathematical landmarks may be locally central, bridge-like, statement-aligned, or structurally distinctive even when not globally central.
- Definitions, theorems, proofs, and exact expressions have different roles but belong to one linked formal structure.
- The theorem interface, proof certificate, local use event, dynamic applicability, and semantic relevance are distinct relations.
- New verified objects change the future navigation geometry.

## Expected outcome range

A plausible positive result is not perfect separation. It is:

- certain P3 classes being highly structurally recognizable;
- other classes remaining ambiguous;
- a calibrated soft infrastructure prior;
- and modest theorem-local ranking gains when global role is combined with local proof structure.

A plausible negative result is equally informative:

- global topology may mostly recover degree, import boundaries, or library engineering conventions;
- landmarks may require text, semantics, local proof states, or explicit use histories;
- P3’s broad machinery/content split may be too coarse.

The study should distinguish these possibilities.
